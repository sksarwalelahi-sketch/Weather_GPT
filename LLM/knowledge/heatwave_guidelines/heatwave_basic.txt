HEATWAVE SAFETY GUIDELINES

A heatwave is a period of unusually high temperatures that can create
health and safety risks, especially when high temperatures continue for
several days.

DURING A HEATWAVE

1. Drink water regularly throughout the day.
2. Avoid unnecessary outdoor activities during the hottest part of the day.
3. Wear lightweight and loose-fitting clothing.
4. Stay in cool, shaded, or well-ventilated places whenever possible.
5. Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight.
6. Check on elderly people, children, and other people who may be more
   vulnerable to extreme heat.
7. Follow official heatwave warnings and local authorities' advice.

OUTDOOR ACTIVITIES

1. Try to schedule outdoor work during cooler parts of the day.
2. Take regular breaks in a shaded or cool location.
3. Drink sufficient water during physical activity.
4. Avoid staying in direct sunlight for long periods.
5. Follow local heat and weather warnings.

HEATWAVE PREPAREDNESS

1. Keep drinking water available.
2. Keep rooms properly ventilated.
3. Keep curtains or shades closed when direct sunlight enters the room.
4. Keep necessary emergency contacts available.
5. Monitor official weather forecasts during periods of extreme heat.

WARNING SIGNS

Extreme heat can cause serious problems such as dehydration and
heat-related illness. If someone becomes seriously unwell during
extreme heat, seek appropriate medical assistance immediately.

IMPORTANT

Heatwave forecasts and warnings should be obtained from official weather
and government authorities. WeatherGPT provides general heat-safety
information and should not replace professional medical advice or
official emergency instructions.
