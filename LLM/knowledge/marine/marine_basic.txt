MARINE WEATHER GUIDELINES

Marine weather information is important for the safety of ships, boats,
fishing activities, coastal operations, and other marine activities.

IMPORTANT MARINE WEATHER PARAMETERS

1. Wind speed
2. Wind direction
3. Wave conditions
4. Wave height
5. Visibility
6. Rainfall
7. Storm conditions
8. Sea conditions
9. Weather warnings

STRONG WINDS

Strong winds can create dangerous sea conditions and high waves.

Marine operators should monitor official marine weather forecasts and
warnings before travelling or conducting activities at sea.

HIGH WAVES

High waves can make marine travel dangerous, especially for small boats
and fishing vessels.

Operators should check official wave and sea-condition forecasts before
going to sea.

STORMS

Storms can produce strong winds, heavy rainfall, high waves, and poor
visibility.

People operating vessels should follow official marine warnings and
instructions from relevant authorities.

POOR VISIBILITY

Fog, heavy rain, storms, and other weather conditions can reduce
visibility at sea.

Marine operators should monitor visibility conditions and official
marine weather reports.

FISHING ACTIVITIES

Weather information can help fishermen and marine operators decide when
conditions are suitable for marine activities.

Before going to sea, they should check:

- Wind speed
- Wind direction
- Wave height
- Sea conditions
- Rainfall
- Storm warnings
- Visibility
- Official marine warnings

COASTAL SAFETY

People near coastal areas should follow official weather and disaster
management warnings during severe weather conditions.
