AGRICULTURE WEATHER GUIDELINES

Weather conditions can strongly affect farming activities, crop growth,
irrigation, harvesting, and livestock management.

WEATHER INFORMATION IMPORTANT FOR FARMERS

1. Temperature
2. Rainfall
3. Rainfall probability
4. Humidity
5. Wind speed
6. Weather warnings
7. Forecast for upcoming days

RAINFALL AND FARMING

1. Rainfall forecasts can help farmers plan irrigation.
2. Heavy rainfall may create waterlogging and crop damage.
3. Farmers should monitor rainfall warnings before applying fertilizers
   or pesticides.
4. During periods of expected heavy rain, farmers should follow local
   agricultural advisories.
5. Farmers should avoid unnecessary irrigation when sufficient rainfall
   is expected.

EXTREME HEAT

1. High temperatures can increase water requirements for crops.
2. Farmers should monitor official heat and weather warnings.
3. Adequate water management can help reduce heat stress on crops.
4. Livestock should have access to sufficient drinking water and shade.

STRONG WINDS

1. Strong winds can damage crops, trees, greenhouses, and farm structures.
2. Farmers should secure temporary structures when strong winds are expected.
3. Follow official weather warnings before performing outdoor agricultural
   activities.

FARMING DECISIONS

Weather information can help farmers decide:

- When to irrigate
- When to harvest
- When to sow crops
- When to apply fertilizers
- When to apply pesticides
- When to protect crops from extreme weather
- When to take precautions for livestock

IMPORTANT

Agricultural decisions should consider local soil conditions, crop type,
growth stage, local weather conditions, and advice from agricultural
experts.

WeatherGPT provides general weather-based agricultural information.
Farmers should follow official agricultural advisories and local
government recommendations for important farming decisions.
