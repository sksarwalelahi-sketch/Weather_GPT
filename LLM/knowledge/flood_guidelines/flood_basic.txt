FLOOD SAFETY GUIDELINES

A flood occurs when water covers land that is normally dry. Flooding can
happen because of heavy rainfall, overflowing rivers, storms, coastal
events, or poor drainage.

BEFORE A FLOOD

1. Monitor official weather and flood warnings.
2. Keep emergency supplies, drinking water, food, and basic medicines ready.
3. Keep important documents in waterproof containers.
4. Charge mobile phones and emergency power banks.
5. Move valuable items and electrical equipment to higher locations.
6. Know the safest evacuation route in your area.
7. Follow evacuation instructions issued by local authorities.

DURING A FLOOD

1. Move to higher and safer ground when instructed.
2. Do not walk or drive through floodwater.
3. Avoid bridges and roads covered by flowing water.
4. Stay away from electrical wires and electrical equipment exposed to water.
5. Keep listening to official emergency announcements.
6. If authorities order evacuation, leave the area immediately using the
   recommended route.
7. Do not attempt to cross fast-moving water.

AFTER A FLOOD

1. Return home only when authorities say that it is safe.
2. Avoid standing floodwater because it may contain contaminants.
3. Stay away from damaged buildings.
4. Avoid fallen electrical wires.
5. Do not use electrical equipment that has been exposed to water until
   it has been checked by a qualified professional.
6. Continue monitoring official weather and disaster-management updates.

FLOOD WARNING

If heavy rainfall or rising water creates a flood risk, people should
follow warnings from official weather and disaster-management authorities.

IMPORTANT

Flood forecasts and emergency warnings should be obtained from official
weather and government authorities. WeatherGPT provides general
preparedness information and should not replace official emergency
instructions.
