CYCLONE SAFETY GUIDELINES

A cyclone is a large weather system with strong winds and heavy rainfall.

CYCLONE ALERT LEVELS

Cyclone Watch:
A cyclone may develop or affect an area. People should monitor official weather updates.

Cyclone Warning:
A cyclone is expected to affect the area. People should follow instructions from local authorities.

BEFORE A CYCLONE

1. Monitor official weather and government warnings.
2. Keep drinking water and essential medicines ready.
3. Keep important documents in a safe and waterproof place.
4. Charge mobile phones and emergency power banks.
5. Secure loose objects around the house.
6. Follow evacuation instructions when authorities issue them.

DURING A CYCLONE

1. Stay indoors and away from windows.
2. Do not go outside during strong winds or heavy rain.
3. Follow instructions from local authorities.
4. Avoid flooded roads and flowing water.
5. Keep emergency communication devices available.

AFTER A CYCLONE

1. Do not enter damaged buildings until they are declared safe.
2. Avoid fallen electrical wires.
3. Avoid contaminated floodwater.
4. Continue following official weather and disaster-management updates.

IMPORTANT

Weather forecasts and cyclone warnings should be obtained from official weather and government authorities. The WeatherGPT knowledge base provides general safety information and should not replace official emergency instructions.
