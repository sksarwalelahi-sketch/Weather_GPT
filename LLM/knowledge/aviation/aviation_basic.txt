AVIATION WEATHER GUIDELINES

Weather conditions are important for aviation safety and flight operations.

IMPORTANT AVIATION WEATHER PARAMETERS

1. Wind speed
2. Wind direction
3. Visibility
4. Cloud conditions
5. Precipitation
6. Thunderstorms
7. Temperature
8. Pressure
9. Weather warnings

VISIBILITY

Poor visibility caused by fog, heavy rain, dust, or other conditions can
affect aircraft operations.

Pilots and aviation operators should monitor official aviation weather
information before and during flight operations.

STRONG WINDS

Strong winds can affect takeoff, landing, and aircraft operations.

Wind direction and wind speed should be monitored using official aviation
weather information.

THUNDERSTORMS

Thunderstorms can produce strong winds, heavy rainfall, lightning, and
turbulence.

Aircraft operators should monitor official weather warnings and aviation
weather reports when thunderstorms are expected.

HEAVY RAIN

Heavy rainfall can reduce visibility and affect airport and flight
operations.

Official aviation weather information should be checked before making
operational decisions.

AVIATION WEATHER INFORMATION

Important information may include:

- Current weather conditions
- Wind information
- Visibility
- Cloud conditions
- Precipitation
- Thunderstorm warnings
- Forecast conditions
- Airport weather information
